# Voice and attribution

## Summary
Ang's README and docs speak in Zac's own voice and credit him and We Right Code without fuss. Every fact here is the user's statement of intent (ET 1, evidence tier 1: a primary source), given at bootstrap on 2026-11-13. The website status is dated and will change.

## Voice
- Zac's natural voice comes through prominently: conversational, opinionated, humorous, with a bit of whimsy.
- Trim his verbosity so readers aren't flooded, but keep the spirit and the way he explains ideas.
- Not corporate. Ang is one developer's project and doesn't pretend to the standards of React-scale repos.
- Avoid generic AI-generated design and copy.

## Attribution
- Briefly mention that Ang was developed by Zac Braddy as part of his work under **We Right Code**, linking to **https://zackerthehacker.com** (his CV site). The mention should be easy to find, never pushed in readers' faces.
- Ang doubles as a portfolio piece for Zac and We Right Code.
- We Right Code has no website as of 2026-11-13, so there is nothing to link to for it yet.
