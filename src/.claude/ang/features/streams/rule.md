## Streams
The user may run several Ang sessions in parallel, one stream (branch and worktree) each; the banner names this checkout's stream. The registry and mailbox live on the `ang/streams` branch: use `ang-context-manager.mjs stream …` and `mail …`, never check it out. Conventions: `.claude/ang/features/streams/conventions.md`.
- **No mail is the normal outcome.** Every session in the recipient stream reads a note until it is handled, so an irrelevant note costs more than a missed one: the recipient can always peek. Suggest a note only at a skill's mail checkpoint, and only when the news would change what another stream does or decides within its focus (`stream list`). Never mail routine progress, small changes or anything a peek would find. At most one note per checkpoint; show its text, target and why, and `mail send` only after the user says yes.
- **Unread mail waits for the user.** When the banner shows unread mail, mention it once in a line ("2 notes waiting, `/read-mail` when you're ready") and carry on with what they opened the session for. When they ask in any words to read, deal with or go through the mail, follow `.claude/ang/features/streams/read-mail.md`.
- **Another stream's context** comes through `context-scout`, which can read other streams' branches without checking them out.
